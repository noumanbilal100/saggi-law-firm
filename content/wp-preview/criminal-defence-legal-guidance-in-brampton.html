Important Information

Understanding the Criminal Justice Process

The criminal justice process may involve arrest, charges, release conditions, a bail hearing, disclosure, pre-trial discussions, applications, trial, sentencing, and possible appeals. Not every case follows the same path.

For example, some cases may be resolved without a trial, while others require extensive preparation for court. Matters involving indictable offences can carry particularly serious consequences and may require detailed preparation and experienced defence counsel.

Canadian criminal law is supported by federal institutions and legislation, including the Federal Department of Justice, while appeals and significant legal questions can ultimately reach courts such as the Supreme Court of Canada. Your lawyer's role is to explain how the law applies to your particular circumstances and help you make informed decisions.
