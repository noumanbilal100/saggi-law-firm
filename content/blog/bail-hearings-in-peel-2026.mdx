The Supreme Court's decision in *R. v. Antic* recentred the bail conversation on the ladder principle — release on the least onerous condition first. In practice, at busy courthouses like Brampton, sureties are still routinely proposed and still often expected.

## When a surety helps

A surety is a person who pledges money and undertakes supervision. On serious charges, or where the accused has a criminal record, a well-prepared surety plan often turns a detention motion into a release order.

## What a good release plan looks like

- One or two named sureties with the ability to supervise.
- Realistic, enforceable conditions (curfew, no-contact, residence).
- A plan for how bail money will be paid if forfeited.
